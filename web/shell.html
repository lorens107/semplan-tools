Only offered for curricula with real week-by-week source data (right now: Beyond the Page ELA and Zearn Math, in Grades 1, 2 and 3). The rest of the year slides back to match, and anything that runs past week 36 doesn't fit this year — same as any curriculum that finishes early.

Before the first paste
  • Paste normally with Ctrl+V. That is what carries the line breaks inside a cell and the links on the activity names. Paste values only drops both.
  • Click the single cell named on the button, not the whole range. The paste fills down and right from there.
  • Column C is never copied. Those are the CT Approval checkboxes, and a paste over them turns them into the word FALSE.
  • The paste carries the plan's own cell formatting — Arial 10, wrapped, top-aligned, the blue fill. Check the first block before doing the rest, and undo if your sheet is set up differently.
